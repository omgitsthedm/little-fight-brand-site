# Little Fight NYC — Website Design Mockup

> **The Ritz-Carlton of Tech Support**  
> A 5-star digital experience for small business owners who've been burned by big agencies and confusing tech.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

**Inspiration**: Apple Store + Ritz-Carlton concierge service  
**Vibe**: Premium, approachable, urgent-but-not-panicked  
**Tone**: "We're here to help. No judgment. Just results."

### Color Palette

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| **Primary Orange** | `#FE5900` | CTAs, accents, highlights |
| **Orange Light** | `#FF7A2F` | Gradients, hover states |
| **Bone** | `#F6F3EE` | Primary background |
| **Charcoal** | `#26231F` | Dark sections, footer |
| **Graphite** | `#1B1B1B` | Primary text |
| **Muted** | `#6B6560` | Secondary text |
| **Stone** | `#CFC6BB` | Borders, dividers |

### Typography

- **Headlines**: Space Grotesk, 600 weight, -0.03em tracking
- **Body**: Inter, 400 weight, 1.7 line-height
- **Eyebrows**: Inter, 700 weight, uppercase, 0.2em letter-spacing

## ✨ Animation Specifications

### Hero Section
- **Eyebrow**: Fade in, 0.6s delay
- **Title words**: Staggered reveal, 0.1s between each, 0.4s start delay
- **Subtitle**: Fade up, 0.8s delay
- **CTAs**: Fade up, 1s delay
- **Stats cards**: Fade up, 1.2s delay, 0.15s stagger

### Scroll-Triggered
- **All sections**: Fade up + translate Y (50px → 0)
- **Cards**: Stagger 0.1s between items
- **Images**: Scale 1.08 on hover
- **Buttons**: Shimmer effect on hover

### Background
- **Floating orbs**: Continuous slow drift (20-30s loop)
- **Opacity pulse**: 3s ease-in-out infinite
- **Grid pattern**: Static, 2% opacity
